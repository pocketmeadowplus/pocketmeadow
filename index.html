A meadow in your pocket

Pocket Meadow is a collection of small, calm nature places for iPhone. Let a worry float away on a leaf, burn a note by candlelight, rake a zen garden, grow a gratitude garden, or breathe with the sky until the storm passes.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Everything you write stays on your phone.

Support

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea for a new calm place? Email pocketmeadowplus@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 days.

I bought Pocket Meadow Plus but places are still locked.

Open the app, tap the gear on the home screen, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I don't hear any sound.

Check that your iPhone's volume is up. On the home screen, tap the round speaker button and make sure sound is on. There you can also change the volume and turn vibration on or off.

How do I make places move slower or faster?

On the home screen, tap the round Pace button and choose Slow, Normal or Lively. Every place follows your choice.

Where are the calm sounds?

On the home screen, tap Calm sounds, the first card under "What do you need right now?". Mix rain, fireplace, forest wind, ocean and birds.

Blow Away the Clouds, Pinwheel or Wind Turbine does not react to my breath.

Go to iPhone Settings → Pocket Meadow and turn on Microphone. Blow gently toward the bottom of your phone, where the microphone is. Talking does not count — only a real breath out. You can also press and hold the round button to blow.

How do I let go of a photo?

Tap the camera button next to the text box (or at the bottom of Foggy Window). Take a photo or choose one from your library. Photos are never uploaded. Only Gratitude Garden keeps a photo with its flower, on your phone, so you can see it again.

The camera does not open.

Go to iPhone Settings → Pocket Meadow and turn on Camera. You can always choose a photo from your library instead.

Will I lose my garden or constellations?

They are saved on your phone and stay when you update the app. They are removed if you delete the app.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
