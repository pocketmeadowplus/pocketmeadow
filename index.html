A meadow in your pocket

Pocket Meadow is a collection of small, calm nature places for iPhone. Let a worry float away on a leaf, burn a note by candlelight, rake a zen garden, grow a gratitude garden, or breathe with the sky until the storm passes.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Everything you write stays on your phone.

Support

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea for a new calm place? Email pocketmeadowplus@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 days.

I bought Pocket Meadow Plus but places are still locked.

Open the app, tap the gear on the home screen, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I don't hear any sound.

Check that your iPhone's silent switch is off and the volume is up. In the app, tap the gear and make sure Nature sounds is on.

"Blow Away the Clouds" does not react to my breath.

Go to iPhone Settings → Pocket Meadow and turn on Microphone.

Will I lose my garden or constellations?

They are saved on your phone and stay when you update the app. They are removed if you delete the app.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
